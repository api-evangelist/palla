# Palla

> Palla Financial is a fintech infrastructure company powering instant cross-border peer-to-peer (P2P) money transfers to 180+ countries. The Palla Platform Partner API lets trusted partners embed transfers, manage a user's account, payment methods, relationship links, and transfers, and receive transfer callbacks. Backed by Cowboy Ventures.

## APIs
- [Palla Platform Partner API](https://docs.platform.palla.app): Partner API for P2P cross-border transfers (base https://api.platform.palla.app, versioned /v1)

## Specs
- [OpenAPI (derived)](openapi/palla-platform-openapi.yml): 13 operations across Auth, Accounts, Payment Methods, Links, Relationships, Transfers
- [Webhooks](asyncapi/palla-webhooks.yml): transfers.create server-to-server callback
- [Authentication](authentication/palla-authentication.yml): client_credentials exchange -> user-scoped Bearer JWT
- [Conventions](conventions/palla-conventions.yml): idempotency, limit/offset pagination, x-palla-request-id tracing, meta/error envelope
- [Error catalog](errors/palla-problem-types.yml): meta/error envelope with numeric rc codes
- [Data model](data-model/palla-data-model.yml): Account, PaymentMethod, Link, Relationship, Transfer
- [Conformance](conformance/palla-conformance.yml): OAuth2, PCI DSS, SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, OFAC
